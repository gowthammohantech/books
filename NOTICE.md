## Illustrations

The empty-state artwork in `apps/web/src/assets/illustrations/` is from
[Storyset](https://storyset.com) (Freepik Company S.L.), used under the
Storyset free licence, which permits commercial use and modification and
requires attribution:

> Illustrations by [Storyset](https://storyset.com)

All ten are the "rafiki" style. They are modified from their originals: the
baked backgrounds are removed and the accent colour is changed to the Elixir
Books brand primary. The transformation is reproducible — see
`apps/web/scripts/fetch-illustrations.mjs`, which fetches each source below and
rewrites it.
